Dissolved CO2 & H2CO3
• Dissolved CO2(aq)
• Gaseous CO2 (g)
• Carbonic Acid H2CO3(aq)
• H2CO3(aq) << CO2(aq)
• CO2 (aq) and H2CO3(aq) cannot be analyzed independently
• They are therefore lumped and termed CO2* or H2CO3*

Conservative properties

and set by mixing ratio
• pH, pCO2, HCO3- etc are not conservative
