3.2 Gas Solubilities
The most convenient of the numerous deﬁnitions for
gas solubilities, and the one we will use here, is our
restatement of Henry’s law, equation (3.1.2). The sol-
ubility S
A is equivalent to the K0 of Warner and Weiss
[1985]. However, solubilities are commonly reported in
the literature as the Bunsen coefﬁcient b or the solu-
bility function F, so it is necessary to specify how to
convert from these solubility parameters to the pa-
rameter S
A. Panel 3.2.1 explains how to do this. Table
3.2.1 summarizes the necessary relationships derived
in panel 3.2.1, and table 3.2.2 gives equations and co-
efﬁcients for calculating Bunsen coefﬁcients and solu-
bility functions for several important gases.
