North Paciﬁc
Seasonal changes in SST are somewhat larger in the
North Paciﬁc than in the North Atlantic, largely be-
cause North Atlantic SSTs do not get as cold in winter
due to the large northward heat transport in this basin.
This leads to a stronger winter-summer thermal forcing
on pCO
2, with values reaching well above 100 matm
(ﬁgure 8.3.7b) across a wide swath of the high-latitude
North Paciﬁc. Nevertheless, as was the case in the high-
latitude North Atlantic, the surface ocean warming
from winter to summer is accompanied by a reduction
in surface ocean pCO
2.
