Water Column Dissolution
The degree to which a settling CaCO 3 particle get dis-
solved in the water column once it enters undersatu-
rated conditions depends on how fast the dissolution
reaction is relative to the time it takes for the particle to
settle to the bottom of the ocean. For that purpose, we
need to have an estimate of the dissolution kinetics and
of the settling speed.

How does this compare to the time it takes for
CaCO3 particles to settle through the water column?
Because of their high density (see chapter 5), carbonate
mineral particles are among the fastest settling parti-
cles, and sinking velocities of more than 100 m day
/C0 1

short in comparison to the timescale of dissolution.
