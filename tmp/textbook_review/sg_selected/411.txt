In chapter 10, we will also discuss a third type
of CaCO 3 compensation, which occurs as a result of
perturbations of atmospheric CO 2 external to the ocean,
e.g., driven by imbalances in the terrestrial carbon cycle,
or by the release of CO
2 from the burning of fossil fuel.
In this case, the oceanic carbonate system acts as a
negative feedback, neutralizing the carbon that has been
added to the atmosphere-ocean-sediment system.
