What are the processes apart from the saturation
state that control the preservation? Is it the rain rate of
noncarbonate material or the rain rate of carbonate it-
self that inﬂuences the details in the preservation pat-
tern? It turns out that while these two processes do
inﬂuence diagenesis of CaCO
3, there exists a further
major difference between the dissolution of opal and
carbonate. Diagenetic reactions independent of the
diagenesis of CaCO

change the carbonate saturation state of the pore waters
in the sediments independently of the saturation state
of the overlying waters.
